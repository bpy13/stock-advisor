- **Cointegration method (Vidyamurthy 2004 book):** a secondary source says he uses the Engle-Granger two-step test with an ADF test on residuals, notes ordering sensitivity, and frames the model as a VECM; Johansen appears as an alternative [snippet] (search summary only; book not opened).
- **Costs:** Do-Faff one-way frictions 0.81% (1963-88) and 0.33% (1989-2009), plus 1%/year short fee [page, CXO]. The CXO page says individual investors' frictions "would be substantially higher" [page]. Rad-Low-Faff net returns above [snippet].
- **Decay:** Do-Faff 2010: continued decline [page]. Rad-Low-Faff: from 2009 the number of trading opportunities for distance and cointegration fell sharply; copula stable [snippet]. Do-Faff 2012: 1989-2009 profit concentrated in 2000-2002 [page, CXO].
- **Coca-Cola / Pepsi:** I found no peer-reviewed paper naming the pair. Course notes and blogs: a Hong Kong UST course page says the pair was not cointegrated in 2013 [snippet] https://palomar.home.ece.ust.hk/MAFS5310_lectures/Rsession_pairs_trading_with_R.html ; a US course project found the log spread mean-reverting with AR coefficient near one [snippet] https://ionides.github.io/531w26/midterm_project/project05/blinded.pdf ; a Python walkthrough found it unusable in its test period [snippet]. Results depend on sample and test [snippet].
- **Hong Kong / Asia:**
  - Ma and Slepaczuk, Univ. of Warsaw WP 2022-02, https://ideas.repec.org/p/war/wpaper/2022-02.html [page]: correlation, cointegration and distance pairs on 50 Hang Seng stocks, daily 2013-2020; all three "profitable" and beat the market on risk-adjusted metrics; cointegration best; results sensitive to number of pairs and rebalancing period. Page does not analyse transaction costs or short-selling.
  - Zhang and Urquhart, Int. J. Finance & Economics 24(2), 2019, https://ideas.repec.org/a/wly/ijfiec/v24y2019i2p698-726.html [page]: Mainland China + Hong Kong large/mid caps Jan 1996-Jul 2017; pairs within one market: no significant abnormal return; cross-market pairs: 9% annualised abnormal return after risk and costs; post-2012 decline attributed by bootstrap to chance; profit higher in long turbulence. Cost figures not on page.
  - Li, Chui, Li 2014 (Applied Economics Letters 21(16)), 38 A-H dual-listed firms 2009-2013: finds cointegration and error correction; profit figures not seen [snippet] https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:taf:apeclt:v:21:y:2014:i:16:p:1116-1121 ; Econbiz page blocked [page: access denied].
  - HKMA (2007) China Economic Issues: A-share premium over H-share 10% to 260%, market-cap-weighted average over 50%; gaps halve in about 40 trading days [snippet] https://www.hkma.gov.hk/media/eng/publication-and-research/research/china-economic-issues/CEI-200706.pdf
  - A Hong Kong copula study: top-20 pairs max returns 3.39% (distance), 10.95% (cointegration), 26.41% (copula); gross/net not stated [snippet] (source title not captured).
- **Long-only version:** Not found tested in any source above. Each pairs strategy is long-short by construction [page: Gatev "self-financing"; Do-Faff long/short positions]. Zhang-Urquhart and Do-Faff both include short-selling costs or constraints in their designs; neither tests a long-leg-only variant [page / unconfirmed].
