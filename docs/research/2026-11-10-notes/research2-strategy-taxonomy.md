## 2. Data source facts

- **SEC APIs [page]** https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces : submissions API and XBRL companyfacts at data.sec.gov; no key; no rate limit stated on that page (fair-access policy referenced); submissions delay "less than a second", XBRL under a minute; bulk ZIPs nightly ~3 a.m. ET.
- **SEC Financial Statement Data Sets [page]** https://www.sec.gov/dera/data/financial-statement-data-sets : XBRL numbers, quarterly ZIPs, Jan 2009 to Sep 2026.
- **OpenInsider** http://openinsider.com/ : fetch failed (socket closed); terms [unconfirmed].
- **HKEX CCASS shareholding search [page]** https://www3.hkexnews.hk/sdw/search/searchsdw.aspx : terms say info is for "personal and non-commercial use only"; Section 2.3 says you must not "use any programmatic, scripted or other mechanical means" to access this facility. This covers the CCASS facility; the separate Disclosure of Interests (DION) search terms were not read [unconfirmed].
- **hkexnews.hk robots.txt**: returned 404 at both www and www3 hosts [page].
- **HKEX DI filing**: filed electronically via DION system [snippet] https://www.charltonslaw.com/disclosure-of-interests-under-the-securities-and-futures-ordinance/ ; 5% substantial-shareholder threshold and director regime [snippet].
- **SFC aggregated short positions [page]** https://www.sfc.hk/en/Regulatory-functions/Market/Short-position-reporting/Aggregated-reportable-short-positions-of-specified-shares : weekly (Fridays), PDF and CSV, e.g. https://www.sfc.hk/-/media/EN/pdf/spr/2026/10/30/Short_Position_Reporting_Aggregated_Data_20261030.csv ; 2012-2026.
- **HKEX short-selling turnover**: daily after close, designated securities; per-stock history via etnet (third party) [snippet] https://www.etnet.com.hk/www/eng/stocks/realtime/quote_shortsell.php?code=6623 ; HKEX CSV availability [unconfirmed]; HKEX Connect statistics pages failed to load figures [page].
- **HKEX stock options [snippet]**: put/call ratio per underlying, daily trading activity and open positions: https://www.hkex.com.hk/eng/stat/dmstat/oi/oi_o.asp
- **HK fundamentals [snippet]**: no free structured XBRL-level API found; HKEXnews filings are public PDFs; vendors (FinancialReports, EODHD from $19.99, Moomoo API) listed https://financialfilings.com/api-solutions/countries/hong-kong/ ; akshare/yfinance HK fundamentals coverage [unconfirmed].
- **13F**: due 45 days after quarter end (Nov 16, 2026 for Q3) [snippet] https://arkolith.com/blog/13f-filing-deadlines-2026
